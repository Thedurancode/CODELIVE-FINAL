# Real Estate Deal Analysis Guide

## Overview

This guide explains how to analyze wholesale real estate deals to determine profitability and make informed decisions.

## Key Metrics

### 1. Maximum Allowable Offer (MAO)

The MAO is the highest price you can pay for a property and still make a profit. The standard formula is:

**MAO = ARV x 70% - Repair Costs**

Where:
- **ARV** (After Repair Value): The estimated value of the property after all repairs are complete
- **70%**: The standard margin that accounts for profit, holding costs, and closing costs
- **Repair Costs**: Estimated cost to renovate the property

#### Example:
- ARV: $300,000
- Repair Costs: $40,000
- MAO = $300,000 x 0.70 - $40,000 = $170,000

### 2. Return on Investment (ROI)

ROI measures the profitability of a deal as a percentage:

**ROI = (Profit / Total Investment) x 100**

For wholesale deals:
**Wholesale ROI = (Assignment Fee / Earnest Money Deposit) x 100**

### 3. Cash-on-Cash Return

For rental properties, cash-on-cash return measures annual cash flow relative to the cash invested:

**Cash-on-Cash = (Annual Cash Flow / Total Cash Invested) x 100**

### 4. Capitalization Rate (Cap Rate)

Used to evaluate rental property value:

**Cap Rate = (Net Operating Income / Property Value) x 100**

A higher cap rate typically indicates higher risk but potentially higher returns.

## Deal Analysis Checklist

### Property Evaluation
- [ ] Verify property address and ownership
- [ ] Confirm property type (SFR, Multi-family, etc.)
- [ ] Check bedroom/bathroom count
- [ ] Verify square footage
- [ ] Note year built and major systems age
- [ ] Assess current condition

### Financial Analysis
- [ ] Obtain accurate purchase price or asking price
- [ ] Research comparable sales for ARV
- [ ] Get repair estimates from contractors
- [ ] Calculate MAO using 70% rule
- [ ] Determine potential profit margin
- [ ] Factor in holding costs if applicable

### Market Analysis
- [ ] Check days on market for area
- [ ] Research local appreciation trends
- [ ] Verify neighborhood desirability
- [ ] Check school ratings if applicable
- [ ] Research crime statistics
- [ ] Identify any market red flags

## Profit Margins by Strategy

### Wholesale Deals
- Target assignment fee: $5,000 - $25,000+
- Minimum margin: $10,000 recommended
- Best deals: 15%+ below MAO

### Fix and Flip
- Target profit: 15-20% of ARV
- Minimum margin: $25,000+
- Account for 6-12 month holding costs

### Buy and Hold (Rentals)
- Target cash-on-cash: 8-12%+
- Target cap rate: 6-10%
- 1% rule: Monthly rent should be 1% of purchase price

## Red Flags to Watch For

1. **Title Issues**: Liens, judgments, or unclear ownership
2. **Structural Problems**: Foundation cracks, roof damage
3. **Environmental Concerns**: Flood zones, contamination
4. **Zoning Issues**: Non-conforming use, restrictions
5. **Market Decline**: Decreasing property values in area
6. **Over-Renovation**: ARV projections too optimistic
7. **Hidden Repairs**: Electrical, plumbing, or HVAC issues

## Quick Reference: The 70% Rule

| ARV | Repair Costs | MAO |
|-----|--------------|-----|
| $200,000 | $20,000 | $120,000 |
| $250,000 | $30,000 | $145,000 |
| $300,000 | $40,000 | $170,000 |
| $350,000 | $50,000 | $195,000 |
| $400,000 | $60,000 | $220,000 |

## Best Practices

1. **Always verify ARV** with 3-5 comparable sales within 6 months
2. **Add 10-20% contingency** to repair estimates
3. **Know your exit strategy** before making offers
4. **Build relationships** with reliable contractors for estimates
5. **Move quickly** on good deals - they don't last
6. **Trust the numbers** - emotions can cloud judgment
