# Contract References and Samples

## Overview
This document catalogs all contract templates and samples available in the AI agent knowledgebase. These documents serve as references for the Contract Compliance Agent and other AI agents to understand contract requirements, clauses, and structures.

## Available Contract Documents

### 1. Customer Service Agreement (CSA)
**File:** `DispoTree DE CSA.pdf`
- **Purpose:** Agreement between Dispotree and wholesaler for platform services
- **Signed by:** Wholesaler for each LLC they use
- **Frequency:** Once per LLC
- **Key Sections:**
  - Service terms and conditions
  - Fee structure
  - Compliance requirements
  - Data usage policies
  - Dispute resolution

### 2. Xome Auction Services Agreement (ASA)
**File:** `Xome ASA.pdf`
- **Purpose:** Agreement for auctioning properties on Xome platform
- **Signed by:** Wholesaler (property-specific)
- **Frequency:** Once per property submitted to Xome
- **Key Sections:**
  - Property identification
  - Auction terms and conditions
  - Commission structure
  - Marketing authorization
  - Closing procedures

### 3. Hubzu Auction Services Agreement (ASA)
**File:** `Auction Services Agreement V1.docx.pdf`
- **Purpose:** Agreement for auctioning properties on Hubzu platform
- **Signed by:** Wholesaler (property-specific)
- **Frequency:** Once per property submitted to Hubzu
- **Key Sections:**
  - Property details
  - Auction pricing strategy
  - Marketing rights
  - Commission agreement
  - Time frames and deadlines

### 4. Broker Marketing Services Agreement (MSA)
**File:** `Broker MSA Sample.pdf`
- **Purpose:** Agreement between broker and wholesaler for property marketing
- **Signed by:** Wholesaler and state-licensed broker
- **Frequency:** Once per property (Mandatory for ALL submissions)
- **Key Sections:**
  - Broker responsibilities
  - Marketing services provided
  - Commission structure
  - Compliance representations
  - Duration and termination

## Contract Requirements by State

### States Requiring License
**Agreements Must Include:**
- License numbers
- Broker supervision clauses
- Disclosure of licensure status
- State-specific compliance language

States: CA, FL, IL, KY, MD, NE, ND, OK, SC, WA, Philadelphia

### States Requiring Specific Disclosures
**Agreements Must Include:**
- Wholesale disclosure language
- Assignment rights
- Cancellation periods
- Required state notices

States: AL, AZ, MD, ND, OK, PA, SC, TN

## Contract Validation Checklist

### CSA Validation
- [ ] LLC name matches registered entity
- [ ] Authorized signer identified
- [ ] All required fields completed
- [ ] Signature present and valid

### MSA Validation
- [ ] Broker license verified for property state
- [ ] Property address correctly listed
- [ ] Commission terms clearly stated
- [ ] Marketing authorization included
- [ ] Both signatures present

### ASA Validation (Hubzu/Xome)
- [ ] Property details match submission
- [ ] Reserve price specified
- [ ] Marketing rights granted
- [ ] Commission agreed upon
- [ ] Wholesaler signature present

## State-Specific Contract Modifications

### Oklahoma (Effective Nov 1, 2025)
- Must include 2-business-day cancellation right
- Escrow requirements
- No lien prohibition language
- Homeowner disclosure requirements

### Maryland (Effective Oct 1, 2025)
- Seller intent-to-assign disclosure
- Assignee equitable-interest disclosure
- Rescission language
- Specific formatting requirements

### Pennsylvania (Effective Jan 4, 2025)
- Licensure disclosure
- Consumer protection language
- Cancellation rights (30 days)
- Specific broker requirements

### North Dakota (Effective Aug 1, 2025)
- Equitable-interest disclosure
- Profit-intent disclosure
- All parties must receive disclosures
- Cancellation rights for violations

## Contract Generation Rules

2. **Select appropriate state template**
3. **Generate addendum** with required language
4. **Route for e-signature**

### Broker Assignment

- **Verify broker license** is active and in good standing
- **Assign appropriate broker** for property location
- **Include broker information** in all agreements

## Best Practices

1. **Always verify** signer authority against LLC documents
2. **Check state requirements** before generating agreements
3. **Maintain audit trail** of all contract modifications
4. **Use standardized templates** with state-specific modifications
5. **Ensure all parties** receive copies of executed agreements
6. **Store contracts** securely with appropriate access controls
7. **Regularly review** contracts for compliance with changing laws
